Shortcut Integration

Send your daily review directly from Apple Shortcuts.

01Create your Shortcut

Open the Apple Shortcuts app and create a new shortcut. Add one question for each field in your Review Template, then collect the answers as text.

02Build the JSON

Use a Dictionary + URL Encode action. Your fields are matched by ID, so any template works. Older top-level formats are still accepted.


      


      

        
03Connect

        
Copy the JSON schema, then copy your Journal URL and append ?entry= with the URL-encoded JSON. Opening the link saves your entry automatically.
